# Your final project

All example data must be fictional or authorized.

## Problem
Who receives the events? What work should triage reduce?

## Taxonomy
Describe each queue and the insufficiency case. Include five hard examples.

## Questions
Write context, type, instructions, and criteria.

## Policy
Define allowed actions, review, timeout, retries, and return.

## Evaluation
Identify data, reference, partitions, metrics, and acceptance criteria. Separate simulation and real inference.

## Economics
Include input, fallback, review, rework, and amortized deployment.

## Decision
Continue, collect more evidence, or don’t adopt? Justify with results or with the observed gap.

## Rubric
Formulation 20; policy 20; evidence 25; economics 15; reproducibility 20. Target: 75/100 and fixing the essential failures.
